## 1. Event outline (condensed)

**Recap + march [0:02–6:00].** Last time: stage set, Lilly gone, Gulgrim's dagger
in a ransom note pointing at the south gate. Vote: through the gate now (stone
can wait till morning). Water Bear collected (stable animals give berth). Chip
pledges the rescue (party helped him). Rocketcandy proposes no stealth, bear
clears the path. March through the city by scant moon.

**The impaled guards [6:59–10:48].** Gulgrim, out front behind the bear, finds
them: two guards pinned to the gate wall with short swords through their chests.
Investigation 16 (advantage): neither bears the inner-wrist tattoo. Suspicious.
Shade: did Chip share the tattoo lore? Chip: told nothing, been too busy. Dirt
path south: heavy boot prints, multiple, uncountable. Debate (alert the city vs
pursue): pursue, don't risk losing her. Butler theories (recruit him / prove
ourselves / Aaron's movie-logic: faked it to teach a lesson).

**Into the trees [12:39–16:49].** Walls to forest, stream by the road, prints
lost. Darkvision roll call: Derek 60 ft, everyone else ~30. Diamond formation,
Rocketcandy spearheading. First bolt at Keechwah: Dex dirty 20, sidestep, bolt
in the dirt. Keechwah Perception 3: top-right quadrant, nothing more. Weapons
out. Chip's save: 4. Ten piercing plus twelve poison, twenty-two, deep in the
shoulder. The rescue has a casualty before it has a target.

**The blob [19:18–50:15].** Initiative; Vadania first, bow readied on visibility.
Chip goes starry (glow tax accepted), Perception 16: steep downward shoulder hit,
very close, from the left — self-heals 8. Shade into the bush. Bolt vs
Rocketcandy: 17 vs AC 18, dodge. Water Bear sniffs (9, Keen re-roll 10): nothing.
Keechwah Perception 13: from above, close, by the wound's depth — readies
Witch Bolt on sight. Rocketcandy walks to the big tree, yells up, gets silence,
starts climbing (10 ft up, half speed); Perception 14: dark mass, fifteen feet
higher. Then the mass moves: roll 22, acrobatic swing, double-foot kick —
six bludgeoning plus six falling, paladin out of the canopy, prone. Gulgrim
throws the Light dagger (Athletics 10, Heroic Inspiration earned): sticks halfway
up, twenty-foot globe, "aim for that blob." Readied shots trigger: Vadania's
bow 19 for 5 (grunt, stays up); Keechwah's Witch Bolt 22 for 6 lightning — singe
hole through the bark, figure drops, lands on its feet. First visual: a cloak
blacker than the dark around it. Midnight void. Chest emblem.

**Lore bomb [51:45–55:44].** Vadania looks at the emblem and stops. I send the
image to the channel; only Derek understands. Burned village. He remembers.
Constitution dirty 20: he puts it away, keeps his turn, dashes thirty feet
northeast and shouts "Who are you? Identify yourself," bow up, no action left to
use it. The figure crouches. Says nothing.

**Owlbear justice [56:15–1:03:11].** Chip's Starry Wisp 17 (5 + anti-invisible
light) plus bonus arrow 17 (6): hurt, lit, alive. Shade's shortbow from the bush
(17/24, sneak 9+7=16): evisceration-ready. Then the second shooter: 22 on Chip —
seven, eight, and ten poison, twenty-five. Chip drops to zero. The starry light
goes out. Death saves start. And Water Bear — who has spent the session failing
perception checks — puts both paws through the first marksman: 22 for 8, 15 for
11. Eviscerated. Blood on the leaves. The bear stands over the body, and the
forest goes quiet except for dice.

**Triage [1:04:08–1:29:51].** Second-shooter hunt (triangle ping northeast, other
tree, across the stream; fireball jokes, vetoed — rescue target, remember).
Detect Magic: nothing. Healing Word engine (Gulgrim heals Rocketcandy 10 mid-dash
to Chip). Vadania double-dash east. Chip death save 14: alive. Shade 60 feet to
cover. Enemy 19 on Keechwah: twenty damage. Water Bear confirms northeast.
Gulgrim reaches Chip: Cure Wounds L2, sixteen, stable — plus Healing Word on
Keechwah for ten in the same round. Chip stands on half movement, Perception 15
at disadvantage (just died, allowances made): big tree, northeast, 165 to the
middle. Moonbeam, Con save 13: nothing there. Nothing *visible*. Enemy 13 on
Keechwah (AC 12): eighteen plus seven poison, twenty-five. Keechwah down.

**Where is she? [1:32:47–1:38:11].** The north tree shakes — not wind, a scuffle
*inside* it. A black figure gets hurled out onto the rock face, prone, winded. A
second figure lands standing, sword already at the first one's throat. And then
the hood comes into the dagger-light, and the table stops breathing, because it
is Biggins. No butler suit. Studded leather, assassin-cut, moving without sound.
"Where is she? What have you done with her?" The blade presses. A shallow cut,
deliberate, non-lethal. Back to the throat. "I never should have trusted a
cut-rate brand of adventurers like you." And then, to the whole gathered party —
to *us* — Vicious Mockery, four psychic, session over. Gulgrim gets a Healing
Word onto Keechwah for eight just in time to spare him the insult. The rest of
us take it. We earned it.

## 2. Strongest narrative moments

1. **The unmarked dead.** Two guards with swords through their chests and *clean*
   wrists — the tattoo plot's first corpse, and it inverts everything: the ink
   was the only ID anyone trusted.
2. **"Aim for that blob."** A thrown dagger becomes a streetlamp; Heroic
   Inspiration gets invented on the spot. Best DM-improv beat of the journals.
3. **The emblem.** Private lore bomb in a public combat — only Derek understands,
   everyone watches him understand. Vadania's whole revenge arc lands in one
   Constitution save.
4. **Owlbear justice.** Twenty-five damage in, light out, death saves on — and
   the bear, who failed every perception check all night, solves the problem
   with paws. The session's emotional peak is an animal with no lines.
5. **"Cut-rate brand of adventurers."** The butler returns in leather with a
   sword, a prisoner, and a review. Four psychic damage as a cliffhanger.

## 4. Ben's voice — one new note (else per J01–J03)

- **Combat hosting.** This session is one encounter with a march attached. Ben
  narrates like a play-by-play caller who keeps forgetting he's also the
  opposing team: initiative as lineup, readied actions as loaded guns, damage
  totals as headlines ("seven, eight, and ten"). The dice are characters with
  arcs — the dirty 20 that opens, the 22s that wound, the death save 14 that
  holds. Newcomers learn combat by feeling its rhythm, not its rules.

## 5. OPENING (new — written for this book)

> The butler came out of the tree with a sword, and none of us recognized him
> at first, because butlers are not supposed to drop out of foliage holding
> prisoners.
>
> Rewind. Three hours earlier we were standing over two dead guards pinned to
> the south gate with short swords through their chests, and the worst thing
> about them — worse than the swords, worse than the wall — was their wrists.
> Clean. No ink. My party has spent a month treating a wrist tattoo as the only
> identification that matters in Neverwinter, and the first corpses of the
> rescue have bare arms, which means either the conspiracy has a dress code I
> don't know about or these men died for having nothing to do with it.
>
> My name is Ben. I run the game. Last session my players formed a band and
> lost a girl. This session they followed the ransom note south with a bear,
> a diamond formation, and absolutely no plan for fighting in the dark — which
> none of them can see in, except the elf, who can see sixty feet, which is
> somehow worse, because sixty feet of forest at night is exactly enough to
> watch something come for you.
>
> Pour something. We begin at the wall, with the dead.

## 6. SAMPLE — Chapters 1–2 (new narrative, transcript-sourced)

### Chapter 1 — Cut-Rate Brand of Adventurers (recap half; sample ends at the vote)

We open split and close bloody, which for this party counts as a reunion. When
we left them: seven heads around a festival table, one girl gone, one dagger in
one note pointing south of town. Tonight six walk out the south gate
with an owlbear and come back — well. That is the book. Walk with me. South gate.
Moon like a rumor. Bear in front.

The vote takes ninety seconds and tells you everything. Gulgrim: it's the guards
or the butler, either way we go *now*, through the specified gate, immediately.
No time on the note? Then no time to waste. The bard promised the broadcast
stone by morning — morning can wait, the girl can't. Chip pledges himself on the
spot, on the record: the party helped him, now he helps back, which from Josh is
practically a blood oath. Rocketcandy proposes the formation strategy: no
stealth, bear clears the path, diamond around the soft center. Water Bear gets
collected from the stable, where every horse and donkey in the building is
pressing itself into the far fence, because some reputations precede their
owners and some owners precede their reputations. Thinner crowds now — the
Battle of the Bards is ending, lanterns coming down, the festival folding itself
away like it knows something. It does not know anything. It is a festival. But
the party marches through its leftovers feeling watched anyway, and here is the
miserable part: they are right.

**DM NOTE — FORMATION.** *Diamond. Paladin point, bear vanguard, casters inside,
ranger floating. They set this up in ninety seconds with less arguing than it
takes them to order drinks. I have run tacticians for years and never seen a
faster deployment. It will not save them. Nothing saves anyone. But it was
beautiful, and I wrote it down exactly.*
