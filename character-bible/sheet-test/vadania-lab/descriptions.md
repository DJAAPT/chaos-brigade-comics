# Vadania Lab — baseline block for seed-robustness testing

Single block source for `vadania-lab`. Batch 1 is the Arm B Vadania
block verbatim (the starting point, as the Shade, Keechwah, and Gulgrim
labs began from their Arm B blocks). Known failure modes from the bible
NEVER list to watch for: female render, dress, blonde hair (canon is
brown per sheet), "long blonde woman in green" misrender, duplicate
Vadania as barmaid. Sheet-vs-gallery conflict already resolved in the
block: brown hair + green eyes (sheet wins over gallery blonde).

## Vadania (vadania)
