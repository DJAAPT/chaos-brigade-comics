# Gulgrim Lab — baseline block for seed-robustness testing

Single block source for `gulgrim-lab`. Batch 1 is the Arm B Gulgrim
block verbatim (the starting point, as the Shade and Keechwah labs began
from their Arm B blocks). Known failure modes from the bible NEVER list
to watch for: tall, thin, no-beard, missing his heavy furs (furs stay on
in every panel, indoors or out). Canon conflict already resolved in the
block: warhammer (bible wins over the sheet's mace).

## Gulgrim (gulgrim)

adult male hill dwarf cleric, short stocky, broad long white beard, heavy furs, frost-covered boots, warhammer, standing well under five feet tall, broad and compact, holy symbol of Marthammor Du, exactly two arms
