# Keechwah Lab — baseline block for seed-robustness testing

Single block source for `keechwah-lab`. Batch 1 is the Arm B Keechwah
block verbatim (the starting point, as the Shade lab began from its
Arm B block). Known failure modes from the bible NEVER list to watch
for: female render, wings (Verruth confusion — Keechwah has NONE),
hooves, duplicate Keechwahs.

## Keechwah (keechwah)

adult male tiefling warlock, warm bronze-tan skin with a faint red cast, long curly black hair, goatee, large curved bronze-brown horns rising upright side by side from the crown of his skull with bases buried deep in his thick curly hair, solid matte purple eyes with no visible sclera or pupil, male facial features, dark blue/gold robes, gold jewelry, NO wings, sharply pointed canines, a thick tiefling tail behind him, exactly two arms
